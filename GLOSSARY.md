# Economics Observatory search and Newsdesk

Semantic search over the Economics Observatory (ECO) back catalogue, and the Newsdesk: a daily system that watches the ECO homepage and the news, and recommends ECO articles to re-post.

## Articles

**ECO article**:
An explainer published on the Economics Observatory site, usually titled as a question.
_Avoid_: post, piece, answer

**Back catalogue**:
Every published ECO article: what search and recommendations draw from.
_Avoid_: archive, corpus

**Publish date**:
The date an ECO article was first published. It stays the same when the article is re-posted.

## Homepage

**Homepage**:
The front page of economicsobservatory.com: 16 cards in a fixed layout.

**Card**:
One of the 16 boxes that make up the homepage, identified by its number (1–16).
_Avoid_: slot, tile, block

**Fixed card**:
A card that always holds the same kind of content: the Data Hub card, the homepage chart card or the Magazine card.

**Homepage chart**:
The chart shown in the homepage chart card. It links to an ECO article.
_Avoid_: featured chart, pinned chart

**Promo card**:
The optional card below the Data Hub card, linking to a page of the team's choice (often the Playfair Prize). While it is shown, one fewer ECO article fits on the homepage.

**Article card**:
A card showing an ECO article. There are 13, or 12 while the promo card is shown.

**Position**:
An ECO article's place in the order of article cards, from 1 (the lead article) down. Editors set it through each article's homepage position in WordPress.
_Avoid_: rank, slot

**Lead article**:
The ECO article at position 1, shown in the first, largest card.

**Snapshot**:
The homepage as seen at one moment.
_Avoid_: scrape, capture

**Arrival**:
An ECO article appearing in an article card when it wasn't on the homepage in the previous snapshot. An article moving between cards is not an arrival.

**New article**:
An arrival whose publish date is within the previous 7 days.

**Re-post**:
Any arrival that isn't a new article: an older ECO article brought back onto the homepage, at any position.
_Avoid_: repost, resurface, republish

**Working day**:
Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays in England and Wales. Staleness is counted in working days, so Friday to Monday is one.

**Homepage staleness**:
Working days since the last arrival.

**Chart staleness**:
Working days since the homepage chart last changed.

## News

**News source**:
An outlet section whose headlines the Newsdesk reads, such as BBC Business or the FT's UK economy section.

**News item**:
One headline, with its standfirst, from a news source.
_Avoid_: news article, story

**Story**:
A news event, covered by one or more news items, possibly from several news sources.
_Avoid_: topic, theme, cluster

**Priority topic**:
A research area of the LSE Growth Lab. Stories that match one are always included in the digest and highlighted.
_Avoid_: key topic, focus area

## Digest

**Newsdesk**:
The automated system that tracks the homepage, reads the news and sends the digest.
_Avoid_: news recommender, morning automation

**Digest**:
The daily email: homepage staleness, a summary of the economics news, recommendations and key dates.
_Avoid_: briefing, newsletter, morning email

**Recommendation**:
An ECO article suggested for re-posting, tied to the story that makes it timely.
_Avoid_: suggestion, match

**Take-up**:
A recommendation whose ECO article arrives on the homepage within 5 working days of being recommended.

**Key date**:
A scheduled event the digest flags in advance: a significant ONS data release or a central bank's monetary policy decision.
